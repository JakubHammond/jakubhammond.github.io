## Documentation Overview

### Purpose
This website serves as a centralized repository for research and documentation development. It facilitates the documentation of projects, experiments, notes, and work as they progress.

The content reflects ongoing research and active development rather than finalized or instructional material.

### Scope
Topics documented herein may encompass, but are not limited to:
- Hardware description languages (e.g., SystemVerilog)
- CPU and ALU-related concepts
- Experimental projects and prototypes
- Design notes and implementation details

The scope may evolve as projects and research advance.

### Intended Audience
This documentation is primarily maintained for:
- Personal reference
- Research tracking and development documentation
- Sharing work and progress with others who may find it beneficial

Certain sections may assume prior technical knowledge.
