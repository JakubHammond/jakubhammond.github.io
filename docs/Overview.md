# Documentation Overview

## Purpose
This website is used for research and documentation development.
It serves as a centralized place to document projects, experiments,
notes, and work as they are developed.

The content reflects ongoing research and active development rather
than finished or instructional material.

## Scope
Topics documented here may include, but are not limited to:
- Hardware description languages (e.g. SystemVerilog)
- CPU and ALU–related concepts
- Experimental projects and prototypes
- Design notes and implementation details

The scope may change as projects and research evolve.

## Intended Audience
This documentation is primarily maintained for:
- Personal reference
- Research tracking and development documentation
- Sharing work and progress with others who may find it useful

Some sections may assume prior technical knowledge.
